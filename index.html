How Your Membership Works

We Put People First

Finally, healthcare on your terms — access care anytime, from anywhere, 24/7/365. Start with the Redirect Health Member App, available to answer any questions in English and Spanish.

  • Contact us first to initiate any medical need — the Member App is the best way.
  • A medical provider will be recommended, or you can choose your own from a large network. You can even add a doctor.
  • We navigate and coordinate your care, determining if virtual, in-person, urgent, or emergency care is needed.
  • Submit doctor’s bills through the secure Member App — most times we’ll pay your doctor before you get a bill.
