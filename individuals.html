How costs work

Member Responsibility & out-of-pocket

For non-iEverydayCARE services, the Member Responsibility is $2,000 then 20% per plan year for individual plans ($4,000 then 20% for family plans). After the Member Responsibility is met, qualified medical expenses submitted within six months of service are shareable with the Newpath community.

  • Maximum out-of-pocket is $4,000 for individual plans and $6,000 for family plans.
  • Member Responsibility is waived for screening colonoscopies and mammograms.
  • Excludes prescription drug benefits, pre-existing conditions, and is subject to program sub-limits.
  • Emergency room: $500 initial Member Responsibility plus 20% co-share; each additional ER visit adds another $500.

Hospital & Hospital PLUS only

How pre-existing conditions are shared

A condition is considered pre-existing if symptoms or treatment occurred within the 12 months prior to joining. Pre-existing conditions become eligible for sharing based on your tenure with the plan.

Time after membership effective dateShareable
First 12 monthsNot shareable
Months 13–24Up to $25,000
Months 25–36Up to $50,000
Month 37 and afterUp to $125,000 per year

Controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, seasonal allergies and intermittent asthma will not be considered pre-existing when medically managed and stable with generic medications and reported prior to the membership effective date.

Coverage at a glance

What the community shares

Sharing occurs after your Member Responsibility and only when Redirect Health coordinates, guides, navigates, and pre-negotiates your care. Below is a high-level summary — see the Membership Guidelines for full rules, limits, and conditions.

Commonly shared

  • Primary care office visits — no Member Responsibility when guided by Redirect Health
  • Chiropractic — up to 12 office visits per year with no out-of-pocket cost
  • Hospitalization at semi-private room rate (subject to MR and MOOP)
  • Outpatient surgery centers
  • Advanced imaging — $50 Member Responsibility
  • Specialist consults & care — $50 Member Responsibility
  • Physical therapy up to $3,000; occupational therapy up to $3,500
  • Routine adult and childhood immunizations
  • Screening colonoscopy and screening mammogram — without Member Responsibility
  • Tele-counseling / mental health at no cost

Not shared

  • Pre-existing conditions (subject to the waiting-period schedule above)
  • Organ transplants
  • Long-term care & skilled nursing (except Hospital PLUS sharing limits)
  • Fertility treatment and elective sterilization
  • Hospice and hyperbaric therapy
  • Alternative / insufficiently proven practices and nutritional supplements
  • Routine dental and routine vision (medical-only vision to $3,500 per need)
  • Weight reduction, surrogacy, and therapeutic massage
  • Non-medical expenses and late/finance charges

This is a summary only. Many services carry specific limits and pre-approval requirements. See the Membership Guidelines for the complete list.
