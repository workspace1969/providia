Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Providia Health (“Providia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use joinprovidia.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site or submitting information through it, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

Providia Health helps individuals and families explore and enroll in the iEverydayCARE® membership. iEverydayCARE® is managed by Redirect Health for members of the Newpath Medical Cost Share. When you request a quote or enroll, information you provide may be shared with Redirect Health and Newpath Medical, Inc. to process your request. Those organizations maintain their own privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to give us and certain information automatically when you use the Site.

Information You Provide

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Quote and enrollment details such as date of birth, gender, home address, coverage tier, requested effective date, and information about household members you wish to cover.
  • The contents of messages you send us through our contact form.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Basic technical information such as your device type, browser, and pages viewed, collected to operate and improve the Site.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare a quote and process your enrollment request.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To communicate with you about your request, membership, or the plans we offer.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only as needed to serve you or as required by law, including with:

  • Redirect Health and Newpath Medical, Inc. to process your quote or enrollment and administer your membership.
  • Service providers who help us operate the Site and communicate with you, under obligations to protect your information.
  • Authorities or others when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Providia, our members, or the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

  • You may choose not to provide certain information, though this may limit our ability to prepare a quote or process an enrollment.
  • You may opt out of non-essential communications by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails or by contacting us.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you have provided, subject to applicable law, by contacting us.

7. Third-Party Links

The Site may link to third-party websites, including those operated by Redirect Health and Newpath Medical, Inc. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for adults enrolling themselves and their households. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Information about dependents is provided by the enrolling adult.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Your continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our Contact page.

Important Program Disclosures

This program is NOT insurance. iEverydayCARE® is managed by Redirect Health exclusively for members of Newpath Medical Inc., a Wyoming Medical Cost Share organized pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Ann. §26-1-104. Redirect Health and Newpath Medical Inc. are not insurance companies.

This program does NOT meet the minimum requirements for Minimum Essential Coverage (MEC) or the Affordable Care Act (ACA). New Jersey, Massachusetts, Vermont, California, Rhode Island and the District of Columbia have passed their own state-level individual mandate laws that mirror the Federal Affordable Care Act. Medical Cost Share memberships do not satisfy the individual mandate requirements of these states, and it should be expected that state-enforced penalties may apply.

The organization facilitating the sharing of medical expenses is not an insurance company, and neither its guidelines nor plan of operation is an insurance policy. Any assistance with your medical bills is completely voluntary. No other participant is compelled by law or otherwise to contribute toward your medical bills. Participation in the organization or a subscription to any of its documents is not health insurance and is not subject to the regulatory requirements or consumer protections of any state’s insurance code. You are personally responsible for the payment of your own medical bills regardless of any financial sharing you may receive, and if the organization ceases to exist or ceases to facilitate the sharing of medical expenses.

Providia Health helps individuals and families access affordable healthcare through the iEverydayCARE® membership, managed by Redirect Health for members of the Newpath Medical Cost Share.
