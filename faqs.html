Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how iEverydayCARE® and the Newpath Medical Cost Share work. Still have a question? Contact us.

No. This program is NOT insurance. iEverydayCARE® is managed by Redirect Health exclusively for members of Newpath Medical Inc., a Wyoming Medical Cost Share organized pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Ann. §26-1-104. Redirect Health and Newpath Medical Inc. are not insurance companies, and this program does not meet the minimum requirements for Minimum Essential Coverage (MEC) or the Affordable Care Act (ACA).
The Newpath Medical Cost Sharing Community operates as an association risk pool managed by Newpath Medical, Inc., a non-profit Wyoming Health Sharing Ministry organized pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Ann. §26-1-104 and overseen by an independent board. Members agree to work together with Redirect Health to help all members get the right healthcare they need while also paying the right price.
No benefits are paid by Newpath unless the member initiates their healthcare through Redirect Health, whose medical team works closely with the doctor(s) you choose. Sharing occurs after your Member Responsibility is met and only when Redirect Health is used to coordinate, guide, navigate, and pre-negotiate care before it is rendered — or as soon as reasonably possible in an emergency.
There are three iEverydayCARE® plans: Routine Care (Basic), Hospital (Standard), and Hospital PLUS (Expanded). Every plan includes the Everyday 1to1® platform. Hospital adds specialist consults, advanced imaging, hospital care, and emergency room sharing. Hospital PLUS adds sharing for chemotherapy, dialysis, skilled nursing, and air ambulance.
Routine Care starts at $157/month for a Primary Member at any age. Hospital starts at $380/month and Hospital PLUS at $462/month for a Primary Member ages 18–44. Pricing varies by age band and coverage tier (member only, member + spouse, member + child(ren), or member + family). See the For Individuals page for the full pricing table, effective 10/1/26.
For non-iEverydayCARE services, the Member Responsibility is $2,000 then 20% per plan year for individual plans, and $4,000 then 20% for family plans. This is the amount you pay before the Newpath community shares in eligible medical expenses. Qualified expenses submitted within six months of service, after the Member Responsibility is met, are shareable with the community.
The maximum out-of-pocket is $4,000 for individual plans and $6,000 for family plans per plan year. This excludes prescription drug benefits and pre-existing conditions, and is subject to program sub-limits.
A condition is pre-existing if symptoms or treatment occurred within the 12 months prior to joining. There is no sharing for pre-existing conditions in the first year; up to $25,000 in year two; up to $50,000 in year three; and up to $125,000 per year from year four onward. Controlled diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, seasonal allergies, and intermittent asthma are not treated as pre-existing when medically managed and stable with generic medications and reported before your effective date.
Always contact Redirect Health first to initiate any medical need, 24/7/365 — the Member App is the best way. A medical provider will be recommended, or you can choose your own from a large network. You can even add a doctor. The Care Team helps determine whether virtual, in-person, urgent, or emergency care is needed.
Submit doctor’s bills through the secure Member App. Most times Redirect Health will pay your doctor before you ever get a bill. Redirect Health assembles and reviews claims and submits them to the Plan Administrator for payment. Specialist, advanced imaging, and hospital claims must be pre-authorized and coordinated by Redirect Health to be eligible for payment.
Care is guided through the Multiplan Practitioner network, and any doctor who accepts the Redirect Health Usual, Customary & Reasonable (UCR) agreement can be used. You can also add a doctor at least 48 hours prior to a visit. Sharing for specialist care is subject to using a Redirect Health in-network provider.
